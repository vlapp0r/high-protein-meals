Shakshuka is one of those meals that looks way more impressive than the effort it takes. This version clocks in at **32g of protein per serving** and costs around **$2.30 a plate**, thanks to eggs, canned chickpeas, and a little feta doing the heavy lifting. It's ready in 30 minutes and works just as well for dinner as it does for a weekend brunch.

## Why This Recipe Works

Eggs are one of the cheapest protein sources you can buy, and pairing them with chickpeas doubles the protein without adding much cost. The spiced tomato base is deeply flavorful from pantry staples you already have on hand. This is a one-pan meal, which means less cleanup and more time enjoying your food.

## Ingredients

- 6 large eggs
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 oz crumbled feta cheese
- Fresh parsley for topping (optional)

**Makes 3 servings. Estimated cost per serving: ~$2.30**

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until softened.
2. Add the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and chili flakes. Stir for about 60 seconds until fragrant.
3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and stir everything together. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Add the drained chickpeas and stir to combine. Let the sauce simmer for 8 to 10 minutes so it thickens up.
5. Use a spoon to make 6 small wells in the sauce. Crack one egg into each well.
6. Cover the pan and cook on medium-low for 7 to 10 minutes. Pull it off the heat when the whites are set but the yolks are still a little runny. Cook longer if you want firm yolks.
7. Top with crumbled feta and fresh parsley. Serve straight from the pan with crusty bread or pita.

## Tips

- **Bulk it up with cottage cheese.** Stir 1/2 cup of full-fat cottage cheese into the tomato sauce before adding the eggs. It melts right in, adds creaminess, and bumps the protein even higher.
- **Don't skip the simmer.** Letting the sauce cook down before you add the eggs makes a huge difference in flavor. Give it those full 10 minutes.
- **Meal prep friendly.** Make the tomato and chickpea base ahead of time and refrigerate it for up to 4 days. Just reheat, crack in fresh eggs, and you have dinner in 15 minutes.
- **Go easy on the heat.** Cooking the eggs on medium-low with the lid on gives you the most control. High heat will cook the yolks too fast before the whites are fully set.
