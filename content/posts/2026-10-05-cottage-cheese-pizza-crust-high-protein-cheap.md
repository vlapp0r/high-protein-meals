If you love pizza but hate how little protein it gives you for the calories, this recipe fixes that. This cottage cheese pizza crust delivers 28 grams of protein per serving and costs under $2.00 to make. It takes about 30 minutes start to finish and uses ingredients you can grab at any grocery store.

## Why This Recipe Works

Cottage cheese is one of the best protein sources per dollar on the market, and blending it into a crust gives you a firm, sliceable base without any fancy flour or protein powder. Combined with eggs and a little flour, it holds together well and crisps up nicely in the oven. It tastes like real pizza, not like a diet compromise.

## Ingredients

**For the crust:**

- 1 cup full-fat cottage cheese
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour (or oat flour for a slightly healthier option)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/4 tsp salt

**For the toppings (basic, budget-friendly):**

- 1/3 cup pizza sauce
- 3/4 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/3 cup turkey pepperoni (about 1.5 oz)

**Estimated cost per serving: $1.85** (based on 2 servings per recipe, average US grocery prices)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet or pizza pan with parchment paper and give it a light coat of cooking spray.

2. Add the cottage cheese and eggs to a blender or food processor. Blend until completely smooth, about 30 seconds. This step is important because visible curds will affect the texture of the crust.

3. Pour the blended mixture into a bowl. Stir in the flour, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, and salt until a thick batter forms.

4. Pour the batter onto your prepared pan and spread it into a circle about 10 to 12 inches wide. Keep the edges a little thicker so you get a slight crust.

5. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the crust is golden on the edges and set in the middle. It should look dry on top and peel cleanly off the parchment.

6. Pull it out of the oven and add your pizza sauce, mozzarella, and turkey pepperoni.

7. Return the pizza to the oven and bake for another 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly. Slice and serve.

## Tips

- **Blend it fully.** If you skip blending and just mix the cottage cheese in, the crust gets lumpy and does not crisp up properly. Thirty seconds in a blender makes a big difference.
- **Let the crust bake before topping.** Do not rush this step. The crust needs to be fully cooked before you add toppings or it will turn out soggy in the middle.
- **Boost the protein even more.** Swap regular pepperoni for diced grilled chicken or add a sprinkle of hemp seeds under the sauce. Either option adds another 5 to 10 grams of protein with minimal cost.
- **Meal prep friendly.** Bake the plain crusts ahead of time and store them in the fridge for up to 4 days. When you are ready to eat, add toppings and bake for 10 minutes. Pizza in 10 minutes on a weeknight is a win.
