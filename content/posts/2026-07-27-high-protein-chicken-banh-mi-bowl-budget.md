If you love banh mi but hate spending $12 at a sandwich shop, this bowl is your new best friend. It packs 38 grams of protein per serving and costs less than $3 to make at home. Bright, fresh, and filling, this one earns a permanent spot in your meal prep rotation.

## Why This Recipe Works

Ground chicken is one of the cheapest high-protein options at the grocery store, and it soaks up a simple soy-ginger marinade beautifully. Serving it over rice instead of a baguette keeps costs down without sacrificing any of the flavor you love about classic banh mi. The quick-pickled carrots and cucumbers do a lot of heavy lifting here, giving you that punchy, tangy bite with almost zero effort.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground chicken (~$3.50)
- 2 cups cooked jasmine rice (~$0.30)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (~$0.10)
- 1 tbsp fish sauce (~$0.10)
- 1 tbsp sriracha (~$0.05)
- 1 tsp sesame oil (~$0.10)
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated (~$0.10)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced (~$0.10)
- 1 cup shredded carrots (~$0.30)
- 1 cup cucumber, thinly sliced (~$0.40)
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar (~$0.10)
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped (~$0.10)

**Estimated cost per serving: $2.60 (makes 2 servings)**

## Instructions

1. **Quick-pickle your veggies.** Combine the shredded carrots and sliced cucumber in a bowl with the rice vinegar and a pinch of salt. Stir and set aside for at least 10 minutes while you cook everything else.

2. **Mix your sauce.** In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, fish sauce, sriracha, sesame oil, grated ginger, and minced garlic. Set it aside.

3. **Cook the chicken.** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add a light spray of cooking oil. Add the ground chicken and break it up as it cooks. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes until fully cooked through and slightly browned.

4. **Add the sauce.** Pour the soy-ginger sauce over the cooked chicken and stir to coat. Let it cook for another 2 minutes so the sauce reduces slightly and coats every bite.

5. **Build your bowls.** Divide the cooked rice between two bowls. Top each with half the chicken, then pile on the pickled carrots and cucumbers.

6. **Finish and serve.** Scatter fresh cilantro over the top. Add extra sriracha if you want more heat. Serve immediately or pack it up for meal prep.

## Tips

- **Meal prep friendly.** Store the pickled veggies separately from the rice and chicken so nothing gets soggy. Everything keeps well in the fridge for up to 4 days.
- **Swap the protein.** Ground turkey or canned chicken breast work great here if ground chicken is not in stock or is priced higher at your store.
- **Double the pickle.** The quick-pickled carrots and cucumbers are genuinely addictive. Make a double batch and keep them in the fridge all week to throw on anything.
- **Watch your rice.** Day-old rice that has been chilled actually holds up better in bowls and meal prep containers. It keeps the texture from getting mushy when everything is packed together.
