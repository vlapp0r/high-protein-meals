This turkey chili is one of the best meals you can make when you're trying to hit your protein goals without wrecking your grocery budget. Each serving clocks in at **35g of protein** and costs roughly **$2.60**. Make a big pot on Sunday and you're set for the week.

## Why This Recipe Works

Ground turkey is one of the leanest, most affordable proteins at the store, and kidney beans add a serious protein and fiber boost for almost nothing. Everything cooks in one pot in about 35 minutes, which means less cleanup and more time doing literally anything else. It also freezes beautifully, so there's no excuse not to batch cook this one.

## Ingredients

*Makes 5 servings*

- 1 lb 93% lean ground turkey (about $4.50)
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained and rinsed ($0.89)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed ($0.89)
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes ($1.49)
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth ($0.50)
- 1 small yellow onion, diced ($0.40)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced ($0.20)
- 1 tbsp chili powder ($0.10)
- 1 tsp cumin ($0.05)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika ($0.05)
- 1/2 tsp salt ($0.02)
- 1 tbsp olive oil ($0.15)

**Total estimated cost: ~$9.24 / Cost per serving: ~$1.85**

Wait, that's well under $3. You can absolutely grab a bag of shredded cheese or a dollop of Greek yogurt on top and still stay under budget.

## Instructions

1. **Brown the turkey.** Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the ground turkey and break it up as it cooks. Cook until no pink remains, about 6 to 8 minutes. Drain any excess liquid.

2. **Cook the aromatics.** Push the turkey to the side and add the diced onion to the pot. Cook for 3 minutes until softened, then stir in the garlic and cook for another 60 seconds.

3. **Add the spices.** Sprinkle in the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and salt. Stir everything together and let the spices toast for about 30 seconds. This step makes the flavor way deeper.

4. **Add the tomatoes and broth.** Pour in the crushed tomatoes and chicken broth. Stir to combine and scrape up anything stuck to the bottom of the pot.

5. **Add the beans.** Stir in the kidney beans and black beans.

6. **Simmer.** Bring the chili to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Let it simmer uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chili thickens up nicely.

7. **Taste and adjust.** Give it a taste. Add more salt, chili powder, or a splash of hot sauce if you want more heat. Serve hot or let it cool for meal prep containers.

## Tips

- **Double the batch and freeze half.** This chili freezes perfectly for up to 3 months. Portion it into zip-lock bags or freezer-safe containers and you've got future meals locked in.
- **Boost protein even more.** Stir in a scoop of unflavored protein powder at the end of cooking. It sounds weird but you genuinely cannot taste it, and it adds about 20g of protein to the whole pot.
- **Add bulk for fewer calories.** Toss in a cup of frozen corn or a diced zucchini with the beans. It stretches the recipe and adds volume without adding much cost.
- **Swap the turkey if needed.** Extra-lean ground beef or ground chicken works just as well here. Just watch the fat content if you're tracking macros closely.
