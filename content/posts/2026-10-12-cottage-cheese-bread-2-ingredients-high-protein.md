Two ingredients, one loaf, and over 25 grams of protein per serving. This cottage cheese bread is one of the easiest high-protein recipes you can make at home, and it costs less than a dollar per serving. If you've been spending money on fancy protein bread at the grocery store, stop.

## Why This Recipe Works

Cottage cheese is loaded with casein protein and adds moisture to the bread without any extra fat or flour. Eggs bind everything together and add even more protein, so you get a dense, sliceable loaf with a serious nutritional punch. There's no yeast, no kneading, and no waiting around.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups full-fat cottage cheese (16 oz)
- 4 large eggs

**Cost per serving: ~$0.75**

That's it. Buy a 32 oz tub of cottage cheese and a dozen eggs, and you can make this twice over for around $6 total.

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a standard 9x5 inch loaf pan with a little cooking spray or line it with parchment paper.

2. Add the cottage cheese and eggs to a blender or food processor. Blend on high for about 30 seconds until the mixture is completely smooth. No lumps.

3. Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan. It will look thin, but it firms up beautifully in the oven.

4. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes. The top should be deep golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center should come out clean.

5. Let the loaf cool in the pan for 15 minutes before removing it. This step matters. If you cut into it too early, it will fall apart.

6. Slice into 8 equal pieces and serve. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.

## Tips

- **Use full-fat cottage cheese.** Low-fat versions have more water and can make the loaf gummy in the center. Full-fat gives you better texture and more satiety per slice.
- **Blend it smooth.** If you skip the blender and just mix by hand, you'll get a lumpy, uneven loaf. A smooth batter means a consistent slice every time.
- **Toast it before eating.** This bread is good straight from the fridge, but toasting it transforms the texture. It gets crispy on the outside and stays soft in the middle.
- **Add seasoning if you want variety.** Garlic powder, everything bagel seasoning, or Italian herbs can all go directly into the batter before baking. The base recipe is neutral enough to go sweet or savory.
