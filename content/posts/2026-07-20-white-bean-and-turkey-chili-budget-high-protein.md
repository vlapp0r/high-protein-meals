If you want a filling, protein-packed meal that doesn't wreck your grocery budget, this white bean and turkey chili is your new go-to. Each serving clocks in at **32 grams of protein** for about **$2.30**, and the whole pot comes together in under 30 minutes. Meal prep dream, honestly.

## Why This Recipe Works

Ground turkey is one of the leanest, most affordable proteins you can buy, and canned white beans add a serious protein and fiber boost without adding much cost at all. The combination gives you a thick, hearty chili that actually keeps you full for hours. It also reheats perfectly, so it works great for lunch or dinner throughout the week.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey (93% lean)
- 2 cans (15 oz each) white beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes with green chiles
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional toppings: plain Greek yogurt, shredded cheese, green onions

**Makes 4 servings. Estimated cost per serving: ~$2.30**

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until softened.
2. Add the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds, stirring so it doesn't burn.
3. Add the ground turkey. Break it up with a spoon and cook until no pink remains, about 6 to 7 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika.
4. Pour in the diced tomatoes with chiles and the chicken broth. Stir to combine.
5. Add the white beans. Stir everything together and bring the pot to a gentle simmer.
6. Simmer uncovered for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chili thickens up to your liking.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve hot with your toppings of choice.

## Tips

- **Smash some beans for extra thickness.** Before adding the beans to the pot, mash about a quarter of them with a fork. This naturally thickens the chili without adding anything extra.
- **Buy ground turkey in bulk when it's on sale.** Portion it into 1 lb bags and freeze. It thaws overnight in the fridge and saves you real money over time.
- **Swap chicken broth for water in a pinch.** You won't lose much flavor here since the spices and turkey carry the dish. It'll save you a few cents per serving too.
- **Greek yogurt instead of sour cream.** It's a simple swap that adds extra protein and cuts saturated fat. A 2 tbsp dollop on top adds about 3 more grams of protein for almost no cost.
