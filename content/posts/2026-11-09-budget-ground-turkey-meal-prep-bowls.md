If you want a week of high-protein lunches without blowing your grocery budget, this is the recipe to bookmark. These ground turkey meal prep bowls come in at around $2.40 per serving and deliver 38 grams of protein each. Four servings, one pan, about 35 minutes total.

## Why This Recipe Works

Ground turkey is one of the cheapest lean proteins you can buy, especially when you grab the 93/7 blend on sale. Pairing it with rice and black beans stretches your budget further while keeping the protein numbers strong. The seasoning is simple but bold, so these bowls actually taste like something you want to eat on a Thursday afternoon.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs ground turkey (93/7 lean)
- 2 cups white rice, dry
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 can (10 oz) diced tomatoes with green chiles (like Rotel)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: shredded low-fat cheese or hot sauce for topping

**Makes 4 servings. Estimated cost per serving: ~$2.40**

## Instructions

1. **Cook the rice.** Add 2 cups dry white rice and 4 cups water to a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 18 minutes. Fluff with a fork and set aside.

2. **Brown the turkey.** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground turkey and break it apart with a spatula. Cook for 7 to 8 minutes until no pink remains.

3. **Season it up.** Drain any excess liquid from the skillet. Add garlic powder, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir to coat the meat evenly and cook for another minute.

4. **Add the mix-ins.** Pour in the diced tomatoes with chiles, black beans, and frozen corn. Stir everything together and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until heated through and most of the liquid has reduced.

5. **Taste and adjust.** Give it a quick taste and add more salt, pepper, or chili powder if needed.

6. **Build your bowls.** Divide the rice evenly across four meal prep containers. Top each with an equal portion of the turkey mixture.

7. **Store and refrigerate.** Let the bowls cool for 10 minutes before sealing. Store in the fridge for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.

## Tips

- **Buy turkey in bulk when it goes on sale.** Ground turkey freezes well for up to 3 months. Stock up and save an extra $0.50 to $1.00 per pound.
- **Swap white rice for brown rice** if you want more fiber. Add about 15 extra minutes to your cook time and bump water to 4.5 cups.
- **Keep toppings separate** when storing. If you like cheese or avocado on top, add those fresh when you reheat so the texture stays right.
- **Reheat with a splash of water.** Add a tablespoon of water before microwaving to keep the turkey mixture from drying out. Heat for 2 to 2.5 minutes, stirring halfway through.
