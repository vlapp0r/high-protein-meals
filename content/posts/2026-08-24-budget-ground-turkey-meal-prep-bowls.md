If you want a week of high-protein lunches without spending a fortune, these ground turkey meal prep bowls are your answer. Each bowl clocks in at around 40 grams of protein and costs under $3 to make. Prep them Sunday and you're set for the whole week.

## Why This Recipe Works

Ground turkey is one of the best budget protein sources you can buy, especially when you catch it on sale. Paired with rice and black beans, you get a complete amino acid profile plus plenty of fiber to keep you full. The seasoning is simple but bold, so these bowls actually taste good on day four.

## Ingredients

*Makes 5 servings*

- 2 lbs 93% lean ground turkey
- 2 cups dry white rice
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional toppings: salsa, hot sauce, plain Greek yogurt (in place of sour cream)

**Estimated cost per serving: $2.60**

## Instructions

1. **Cook the rice.** Combine 2 cups dry rice with 4 cups water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 18 minutes. Remove from heat and let it sit, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.

2. **Brown the turkey.** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground turkey and break it apart with a spatula. Cook for 7 to 9 minutes until no pink remains.

3. **Season the meat.** Drain any excess liquid from the skillet. Add garlic powder, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir everything together and cook for another 2 minutes so the spices bloom.

4. **Add the remaining ingredients.** Stir in the drained black beans, frozen corn, and diced tomatoes. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the corn is heated through and everything is well combined.

5. **Portion into containers.** Divide the rice evenly among 5 meal prep containers (roughly 3/4 cup cooked rice each). Top each with an even portion of the turkey mixture.

6. **Store and reheat.** Seal containers and refrigerate for up to 5 days. Reheat in the microwave for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring halfway through. Add your toppings after reheating.

## Tips

- **Buy turkey in bulk when it goes on sale.** Most grocery stores run ground turkey deals weekly. Buy two or three pounds, cook what you need, and freeze the rest raw for next week.
- **Swap the rice for cauliflower rice** if you want to cut carbs. It will lower the calorie count by about 150 calories per bowl and is easy to find frozen for under $2 a bag.
- **Double the spice blend and store it in a jar.** Having your own premixed taco-style seasoning saves time on future meal preps and cuts down on the number of containers you dig through.
- **Add a squeeze of lime before eating.** It sounds minor but a little acid brightens the whole bowl, especially on day three or four when the flavors have had time to meld together.
