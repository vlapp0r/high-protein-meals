Dessert that actually supports your protein goals? Yes, it exists. This cottage cheese chocolate mousse comes together in under 10 minutes, delivers 28 grams of protein per serving, and costs less than $2 to make. It tastes like a treat, not a compromise.

## Why This Recipe Works

Blending cottage cheese completely transforms its texture into something smooth and creamy, making it the perfect mousse base. Cocoa powder and a little sweetener do the heavy lifting on flavor, so you are not missing anything by skipping heavy cream or processed pudding mix. It is also meal-prep friendly since it keeps well in the fridge for up to four days.

## Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

- 1 cup full-fat cottage cheese (2% works fine too)
- 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Optional toppings: fresh berries, a few chocolate chips, or a sprinkle of sea salt

**Estimated cost per serving: $1.65**

Cottage cheese is doing the heavy lifting here both nutritionally and budget-wise. A 16 oz tub typically runs $2.50 to $3.50 at most stores, and cocoa powder is a pantry staple that costs pennies per use.

## Instructions

1. Add the cottage cheese, cocoa powder, honey, vanilla extract, and salt to a blender or food processor.
2. Blend on high for 60 to 90 seconds, stopping to scrape down the sides as needed. You want it completely smooth with no curds visible.
3. Taste the mixture and adjust sweetness by adding another teaspoon of honey if needed.
4. Spoon the mousse into two small bowls or ramekins.
5. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes if you want a firmer, thicker texture. You can also eat it immediately if you prefer it a bit softer.
6. Add any toppings right before serving and enjoy.

That is genuinely it. No cooking, no complicated steps, no special equipment beyond a blender.

## Tips

- **Use a high-powered blender for best results.** A food processor works but may leave the texture slightly grainy. If you only have an immersion blender, use a tall narrow container and blend thoroughly.
- **Full-fat cottage cheese gives you the creamiest texture.** The extra fat content helps mimic that rich mousse feel. Low-fat versions work but the result is slightly thinner.
- **Make a bigger batch for the week.** This recipe scales up easily. Double or triple it on Sunday and portion into individual containers for a grab-and-go dessert all week.
- **Boost the protein further with a scoop of chocolate protein powder.** Swap one tablespoon of cocoa powder for a half scoop of chocolate whey and you can push the protein closer to 35 grams per serving. Add a splash of milk if it gets too thick.
