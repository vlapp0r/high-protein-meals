This egg fried rice is one of the best budget meals you can make. It costs under $2 per serving, delivers 35 grams of protein, and comes together in about 15 minutes using ingredients you probably already have on hand.

## Why This Recipe Works

Whole eggs and egg whites do the heavy lifting here on protein without pushing the cost up. Day-old rice is key because it fries up dry and slightly crispy instead of mushy. The soy sauce and sesame oil give you a ton of flavor with almost zero effort.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked white rice, day-old (about 1 cup dry)
- 4 large eggs
- 4 oz 93% lean ground turkey or chicken breast, cooked and crumbled
- 1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 green onions, sliced (optional but worth it)

**Estimated cost per serving: $1.75** (based on 2 servings)

## Instructions

1. Cook your ground turkey or chicken in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat until browned and cooked through. Break it into small crumbles. Set it aside on a plate.

2. Add the vegetable oil to the same pan and crank the heat to high. Add the frozen peas and carrots and stir-fry for 2 minutes until thawed and slightly softened.

3. Add the day-old rice to the pan. Press it into the hot surface and let it sit undisturbed for 60 seconds so it gets a little crispy on the bottom. Then stir and repeat once more.

4. Push the rice to one side of the pan. Crack all 4 eggs into the empty side. Scramble them quickly and start folding them into the rice before they fully set so they coat everything.

5. Add the cooked turkey back into the pan. Stir everything together.

6. Pour in the soy sauce and add the garlic powder and onion powder. Toss well to coat every grain of rice.

7. Drizzle the sesame oil over the top, give it one final stir, and remove from heat. Top with green onions if using.

## Tips

- **Use cold rice.** Freshly cooked rice has too much moisture and turns gummy when fried. Spread hot rice on a baking sheet and refrigerate it for a few hours, or just use whatever leftover rice you have from the night before.

- **Keep the heat high.** Fried rice needs high heat to develop that slightly smoky, restaurant-style flavor. A cold pan will steam the rice instead of frying it.

- **Swap the protein to save even more.** Ground turkey is a solid pick, but canned chicken breast or diced firm tofu will drop the cost per serving even lower while keeping the protein count solid.

- **Double the batch for meal prep.** This recipe scales up easily. Make four servings on Sunday and portion it into containers for the week. It reheats great in the microwave with a splash of water to loosen it up.
