This cottage cheese pizza crust is one of the best high-protein swaps you can make without spending extra money. Each serving runs you under $2 and packs in roughly 30 grams of protein. If you love pizza but keep blowing your macros on delivery, this one is for you.

## Why This Recipe Works

Cottage cheese is one of the cheapest high-protein foods at the grocery store, usually running about $0.20 per ounce. When you blend it with eggs and a little flour, it bakes into a firm, sliceable crust that actually holds your toppings. It is not a cauliflower crust situation where everything falls apart the second you pick it up.

## Ingredients

**For the crust:**

- 1 cup full-fat cottage cheese (blended smooth)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour (or oat flour for a slightly higher protein count)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

**For the toppings:**

- 1/3 cup marinara sauce
- 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 2 oz turkey pepperoni (about 14 slices)

**Estimated cost per serving: $1.85** (based on 2 servings per recipe, using store-brand ingredients)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and give it a quick spray of nonstick cooking spray.

2. Add the cottage cheese to a blender or food processor and blend until completely smooth, about 30 seconds. This step matters. Skipping it leaves lumps that affect the texture of the crust.

3. In a mixing bowl, combine the blended cottage cheese, eggs, flour, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Stir until you have a thick, smooth batter.

4. Pour the batter onto your prepared baking sheet and spread it into a circle or rectangle, about 1/4 inch thick. Keep the edges a little thicker so they hold up like a real crust.

5. Bake for 20 to 22 minutes, until the crust is golden on the edges and firm to the touch in the center. It should not jiggle when you shake the pan.

6. Pull the crust out and add your toppings: marinara, mozzarella, and turkey pepperoni. Return it to the oven for another 7 to 9 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.

7. Let it cool for 3 to 5 minutes before slicing. This helps the crust firm up so it slices cleanly.

## Tips

- **Blend the cottage cheese completely.** A smooth batter makes a cohesive crust. A lumpy batter makes a spotty, fragile one.
- **Do not skip the pre-bake.** Baking the crust before adding toppings is what separates a firm slice from a soggy mess. The crust needs to set before it can support anything on top.
- **Customize your toppings freely.** Ground beef, chicken sausage, roasted veggies, or whatever is on sale works great here. The base crust is neutral enough to pair with almost anything.
- **Meal prep friendly.** Bake two or three crusts on the weekend and refrigerate them without toppings. They keep for up to 4 days. Add toppings and bake for 10 minutes when you are ready to eat.
